Data sources

Every band on the timeline is one of these datasets, imported into one events table by scripts/ingest_events.py. A source is re-synced only when its files change; the same person, team or title in two sources is linked through Wikidata ids, external identifiers or name and dates.

Planned sources

Adapters that exist in scripts/ingest/ but may have no data in this database yet, and datasets on the list. A source above with "not synced yet" only needs its files under data/ and make sync-events.

  • Wikidata dump (wikidata adapter): every person, conflict, country and award with dates, from a reduced latest-all.json. Run make wikidata-extract DUMP=… then make sync-events.
  • Every dated Wikidata item: scripts/wikidata_extract.py --all-dated also keeps any item with a calendar date (inception, publication, point in time) as a stub event under its first class, so books, earthquakes, treaties and launches appear before they get a parser of their own.
  • More Wikipedia lists (wikipedia_lists adapter): make wikipedia-lists SEEDS="List of treaties" fetches any bibliography or "List of …" page; publications become books and articles, every other dated line a stub entry.
  • Candidates: Open Library editions, Billboard charts, Nobel laureates, UN membership, spaceflight launches, football and tennis results.
